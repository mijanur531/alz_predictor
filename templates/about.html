About the Platform

Model Architecture & Prediction Classes

The AlzPredictor model processes patient clinical baselines and lifestyle scores to categorize records into three primary risk categories:

  • Normal: Cognitive performance and physical indicators are in normal ranges. No significant risk is identified.
  • MCI (Mild Cognitive Impairment): Early indicators of mild cognitive changes are present. Patient exhibits subtle deficits requiring monitoring.
  • Dementia: Strong clinical features indicating significant cognitive decline (e.g. low MMSE scores, advanced age, high family predisposition).

Clinical Metrics Collected

Metric Standard Value / Details Impact Weight
Age Years (typically 50-90) Moderate - increased risk in elder classes
BMI Body Mass Index (kg/m²) Low - high obesity adds slight risk factor
Cognitive Score Mini-Mental State Exam (MMSE: 0 - 30) Very High - core indicator of impairment
Family History Predisposition (Yes / No) High - genetic predisposition is critical
Lifestyle Factors Smoking status and Physical activity hours/week Low - modifies baseline vascular risk

Academic Prototype Notice

This web application is built strictly as a portfolio, architectural demonstration, and academic project. It does not replace real clinical examinations. All database records and model outputs are mocks and should not be used as official medical suggestions.
